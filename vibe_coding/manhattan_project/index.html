Model Sources

Park premium (35% at edge, λ=500m):
Nicholls & Crompton (2005), J. Leisure Research.

Decay curve validated in:
Trojanek & Głuszak (2018); Kok, Monkkonen & Quigley (2014).

NYC-specific premium:
Barclays (2018): CP adds ~$15B/yr → ~$500B capitalised at 3%.

Market saturation (elasticity 1.5):
Saiz (2010), QJE: Manhattan housing supply elasticity. Adding ~440M sqft (≈8× existing luxury stock of 60M sqft) crashes $/sqft via P = P₀·(1+ΔQ/Q)−1/ε.

Tourism value ($320B):
NYC & Co.: CP draws ~42M visitors/yr; ~15% of NYC's $75B/yr tourism → $12–15B/yr × 4% cap rate.

Environment & health ($100B):
Urban heat-island mitigation (1–2°F cooler), air-quality, mental-health benefits — Kondo et al. (2018); Gascon et al. (2016); NYC DOHMH.

FAR=12: NYC Zoning §23-151 (R10).
Base $2,750/sqft: Compass, StreetEasy 2023–24.
⚠ Still not modelled: long-run population growth from cheaper housing, crime/density feedback, construction-phasing time value, option value of preserving a one-of-a-kind asset.
