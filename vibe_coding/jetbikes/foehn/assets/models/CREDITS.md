# Models

All but one by [Quaternius](https://quaternius.com), licence CC0 1.0 (public domain), fetched from
[Poly Pizza](https://poly.pizza). The one: [Cattail](https://poly.pizza/m/9vCk81sIPve) by Poly by Google, licence
[CC-BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) (recoloured per corner, like the rest). `sources.json` lists every model with its page and file;
`tools/bake_models.mjs` turns the ones the game uses into `models.bin` (frozen poses, a colour
per corner, no skeleton).

In the game now: the animals (Cow, Bull, black and white Cow, Sheep, Deer, Stag, Horse, White Horse), rocks, bushes, flowers, stumps, logs and hay (`render/flora.js`), and the houses, huts, barns and the well of the valley (`render/props.js`).
Beside the water (`render/flora.js`): three large rocks and the moss rock as the boulders of the springs and the falls, the fern, and the cattail on the slow banks of the river.
